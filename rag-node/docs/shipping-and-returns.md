# Shipping and Returns Policy

## Shipping
We offer free standard shipping on all orders above Rs. 999. Orders below that have a flat
shipping fee of Rs. 79. Standard delivery takes 4 to 7 business days. Express delivery
(2 to 3 business days) is available for an extra Rs. 149. We currently ship across India only.

## Returns
Products can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are unused and in original packaging.
To start a return, contact support with your order ID. Refunds are processed within 5 to 7
business days after we receive the returned item. Sale items and cushion covers are not eligible
for return due to hygiene reasons.

## Damaged or Wrong Items
If you receive a damaged or wrong item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo.
We will arrange a free replacement or full refund. No return shipping charges apply in this case.

## Payment
We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, and cash on delivery (COD). COD is available
for orders under Rs. 5,000.
